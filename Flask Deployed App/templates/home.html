❓ Frequently Asked Questions

📷

What image quality is needed?

+

For best results, ensure: (1) Good lighting without harsh shadows, (2) Leaf occupies 70% of the frame, (3) Clear focus on disease symptoms, (4) Resolution of at least 800x800 pixels. Both JPEG and PNG formats are supported.

🎯

How accurate is the AI?

+

Our model achieves ~92% accuracy on validation data. However, results depend on image quality and disease visibility. For critical crop decisions, please consult agricultural experts.

🌱

Can I use this for indoor plants?

+

This model is specifically trained on outdoor crop diseases for the 14 supported plant species. It may not work accurately for ornamental or indoor houseplants.

❌

What if my plant isn't listed?

+

Currently, we only support the 14 plant species shown above. If you upload an unsupported plant, the system will show an error message with the list of supported plants.

⏱️

How long does analysis take?

+

Image analysis typically takes 2-5 seconds depending on your internet speed and image size. The AI model runs on our servers to ensure fast and accurate predictions.
